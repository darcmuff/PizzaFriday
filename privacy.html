Privacy Policy

PizzaFriday — Pizza Dough Calculator
Last updated: 10 September 2026

Overview

PizzaFriday is a free pizza dough calculator app. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and your rights.

Data Collection

PizzaFriday does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app operates entirely on your device.

Local Storage

The app saves your settings, saved recipes, and session state locally on your device using your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible by us or any third party.

Notifications

If you grant notification permission, the app uses this solely to alert you when a baking timer completes. No notification data is collected or transmitted.

Internet Access

The app loads from GitHub Pages (darcmuff.github.io). No user data is sent to our servers. GitHub's own privacy policy applies to the hosting infrastructure.

Third Parties

PizzaFriday does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. There are no ads in the app.

Children

PizzaFriday is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date.
